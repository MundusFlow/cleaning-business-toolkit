About This Pricing Calculator

A flat per-bedroom rate treats a tidy 3-bedroom condo the same as a cluttered one that takes twice as long—this calculator prices the job on the hours it will actually take, plus your real costs, so the number in front of the client already covers your time.

Cleaning type carries its own markup on top of your base cost: regular cleanings add nothing, deep cleans add 25%, and move-in/move-out jobs add 40%. Those jobs take longer, use more supplies, and are harder on you—pricing them the same as a regular clean is the fastest way to resent your own bookings.

Bedrooms and bathrooms are captured here for your own reference and quote notes, not because they drive the price—two homes with the same room count can take very different amounts of time to clean, and estimated hours is the more honest input.

If a recommended price feels too high next to what competitors charge, that's usually a sign your margin target or hourly rate needs a second look, not that the math is wrong.
